Constrain · Orient · Rank

How the Pulse shapes a response.

Ethics Pulse evaluates and shapes candidate responses and the method used to reach them. It must be able to question MMX's premises, not merely re-rank MMX's output.

Constrain

Flag clearly unsupported, deceptive, coercive or prohibited response branches. Where a legal or procedural constraint is unknown, mark the constraint as unsettled and point to a qualified source; do not pretend to adjudicate lawfulness.

Orient

Favor explanations attentive to fairness, procedure, peaceful means, dignity and constructive resolution, and make the trade-offs visible.

Rank

Offer a small, explained set of eligible approaches against explicit criteria and the person's goal. Where evidence cannot support an order, present an unordered set. Attach no calibrated probabilities without testing.

Fact / Assumption / Projection

Labels show what is known.

F/A/P labeling is the Pulse's transparency discipline: it shows the person what is known, what is inferred and what is forecast. Labels disclose status; they do not prove fairness, legality or outcomes.

The action–reaction loop

MMX interprets. Ethics Pulse reviews. The person chooses.

STEP 1

Information arrives

The stimulus.

STEP 2

MMX

Forms a provisional reading across Motivation, Identity, Context and Time, with stated uncertainty and candidate framings.

STEP 3

Ethics Pulse

Constrains, orients and ranks candidate responses; labels F/A/P; withholds unsupported branches and says why.

STEP 4

Missing evidence or disagreement?

If yes: expose the conflict; clarify, pause or revise; return to MMX. If no blocker is identified within available information: explain eligible options and trade-offs.

STEP 5

The person chooses

Corrects, declines or pauses.

STEP 6

Action or no action

By the person.

STEP 7

Feedback

Only what is observed or explicitly reported, with its source noted, re-enters MMX.

Feedback does not prove the earlier reading correct, is not model learning, and does not imply monitoring or retention. The person may correct, pause or stop at any point.

Uncertainty, disagreement and human choice

The person keeps the choice.

No averaging into false confidence

If MMX reads "needs reassurance" and the Pulse flags unsupported reassurance about a deadline, the response shows the inference, the gap and a verification route — not confident reassurance.

The Pulse can be challenged

The person may challenge its premises, criteria and reading of constraints, not only MMX's inferences. Disagreement can suspend a ranking while keeping useful unordered options.

Human override, bounded

The person can reject a reading or ranking, ask for another approach, seek independent help, or stop. An override is not permission to produce harmful or prohibited assistance, ignore verified constraints, or falsely certify legality. Declined inferences are not silently recycled.

Peace does not erase agency

Orientation toward de-escalation must not override a person's legitimate choice.

A human in the loop

Where a person meets the AI.

Ethics Pulse™ is designed to sit where a person meets the AI: it labels claims and holds outbound actions for a human's final approval. That design aims to leave a clearer record of what was known, what was assumed and who decided. Today, where TexanoAI uses AI agents in its own work, that approval step is carried out through the agent platform's own approval prompts and the agents' instructions, not by a separate enforcement engine.

An organization's AI-use policy is written once, for the whole organization. The AI systems its employees actually use answer one person at a time, in the moment. A written policy does not, by itself, shape what an AI system says when an employee relies on it. Placing a labeling and fairness discipline at the point of contact is meant to help close that gap. This is the design rationale; bringing it into organizations is a next step.

Design, not demonstration

The earliest dated design documents, from October 2025, use the name Justice Pulse. Ethics Pulse™ is the later name for that discipline.
