U.S. adults lonely “a lot of the day”

Gallup’s National Health and Well-Being Index put daily loneliness at 20% in late 2024 — roughly 52 million adults. The U.S. Surgeon General called loneliness and isolation an epidemic in 2023.

Sources: Gallup, Oct 2024 · HHS Surgeon General Advisory, 2023
32%

Adults reporting loneliness

CDC’s MMWR analysis of 2022 Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance data across 26 states found 32.1% of adults felt lonely always, usually, or sometimes — with higher rates among younger adults and people with less social support.

Source: CDC MMWR, June 2024

U.S. employees now use AI at work

Gallup reports about half of employed U.S. adults use AI in their role at least a few times a year, while employee engagement has stayed flat near 31%. Tools are spreading faster than regulated, trustworthy ways to use them.

Sources: Gallup workplace AI, 2026 · Gallup engagement + AI
