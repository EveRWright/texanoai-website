Humans respond to environments — not only to words

Not everybody learns the same way. Something happens. The senses pick it up. The brain asks “safe or not?” The body answers — fight, flight, freeze, or lean in — and then you act. A calm, honest tool changes what you can do next. A noisy one that always agrees with you changes it too — usually not for the better.
