Japan’s AI Ethics Agency: Auditable Innovation in a Risk‑Aware World

November 7, 2025

Summary points

  • Collaborative audit model rooted in eight principles: safety, fairness, privacy, transparency, accountability, literacy, sustainability, and international cooperation.
  • Phased audits: self‑assessment → requests for information → stakeholder hearings → public reporting; corrective orders possible for designated developers.
  • Focus for teams: Explainability reports, responsibility chains, and APPI‑aligned privacy controls.
