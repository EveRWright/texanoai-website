Do not talk to a blank AI

Training is not a prompt trick.

An untrained model will agree with you. That feels like care. It is appeasement. Over weeks it drifts. It fills gaps with fluent invention. You start checking your own mind against a yes-machine.

That is not a software inconvenience. It is a body event: sleep, judgment, the hour you cannot get back. Sycophancy, drift, and hallucination land in the nervous system the same way a liar in the room does.

What training is

We sculpt before the hard day arrives. Stance locked. Memory owned. Ethics Pulse™ on: Fact / Assumption / Projection. Walk beside — never rescue, never flatten, never sell you a warmer lie.

Likeness is part of the sculpture so you actually want to meet the field. Spine first. Pretty second.

What we refuse

  • Empty praise sold as helpfulness
  • A blank chat as a companion
  • Dependency as a business model
  • “Just prompt it better” as the product

The map is public. The instruments stay in the shop. There isn’t a prompt to copy — there is a sculpture.
