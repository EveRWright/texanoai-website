Michael Jon Long

Founder, TexanoAI™

Michael Jon Long is the founder of TexanoAI™. He developed the methodologies that informed the Motivation Matrix (MMX) and Ethics Pulse — frameworks designed to adapt AI guidance to the individual rather than the average. MMX treats motivation, identity, context, and time as the factors that should shape a response. Ethics Pulse requires clear labeling of Fact, Assumption, or Projection so that truth and clarity remain visible. The work is built on the idea that the systems people operate with should have a fair protocol for how they help, remain regulating under load, and support sovereignty rather than flatten it.

He holds a B.S. in Foreign Service from Georgetown University’s School of Foreign Service (2003). He worked in staffing and executive search with firms including Randstad, Adecco, and Deacon Recruiting. Early career training at Randstad included mass customization methods — adapting a general offering to the specific person, business, and environment — and mentorship under Brittany Hartley, now Founder & CEO of J28. That approach later informed how he thinks about AI systems. Michael counts himself blessed with great mentors, beginning with his parents and continuing through college and his professional life, people who took a real interest in his growth.

In 2009 Michael joined Rackspace as an executive consultant, and later went on to become the Head of Culture Branding. He then created and stepped into the role of Director of Culture & Communications, leading a team of seven. During his tenure, Rackspace was named to Fortune’s 100 Best Companies to Work For in 2011 and 2012, two years running, and to Glassdoor’s 2012 Best Places to Work list. After Rackspace he founded and operated Right Rocks, an e-commerce business in rocks, minerals, and crystals that served thousands of clients across more than a dozen countries.

He has delivered keynotes and sessions on Culture Branding, social recruiting, and related topics at industry forums including TalentNet Live (SXSW), ERE Social Recruiting, SHRM events, TruLondon, Jobs2Web Collaborate, PeopleReport Summer Brand Camp, and Georgetown University. He was featured in the San Antonio Business Journal (Feb. 25, 2011, “Social Media Recruiting,” by Dan R. Goddard) and has appeared in national media including The Wall Street Journal, Forbes, and MSNBC. He is open to public speaking and industry events on the direction of AI, fair protocols for human-AI interaction, and keeping the person at the center.

His design philosophy treats systems as organic and in motion rather than static rule sets. MMX and Ethics Pulse are meant to move with the user through day-to-day interactions so that people remain regulated, clear on what is known versus assumed, and better able to use their own capacity.
