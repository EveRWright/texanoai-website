Privacy

TexanoAI treats personal data as belonging to the human — not as fuel for engagement optimization.

We don’t sell your information. We don’t use it to remarket to you.

What you tell us in intake is used to sculpt your companion. That’s the point of the form. A human reads it. Nobody hits “generate companion.”

When a Memory Core is part of the build, it is built for you to hold — not for us to farm.

  • We collect what we need to answer you and to do the sculpt you asked for.
  • We do not sell personal data. Named stories are not published without consent.
  • This is not therapy. You have to be 18.
  • Questions: contact@texanoai.com

This is how we work. We can tighten the legal form as the company grows. It is not a substitute for advice about your own situation.
